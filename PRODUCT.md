# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with TypeScript, npm, and Vue islands only when interaction genuinely requires them. Deployment target: Vercel.

## Users

Primary users are US and Latin American companies seeking a nearby dedicated software team, plus Colombian small and medium businesses with complex operations. They need to understand quickly whether HELIX can solve a specific operational problem and start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

This site introduces HELIX, a Colombian software development team that integrates systems, operational data, and AI into delivery. Success means a qualified visitor understands the kind of operational problems HELIX solves within ten seconds and can schedule a conversation.

## Positioning

HELIX is an operations-focused engineering partner for automation, data and dashboards, custom software, and dedicated nearshore teams. Its differentiator is applying those capabilities to specific workflows in logistics, delivery, workforce management, and contact centers rather than selling generic websites or chatbots.

## Operating Context

The site is evaluated by US and Latin American buyers comparing dedicated nearshore teams, and by Colombian operators looking for practical improvements in complex workflows. Spanish is the primary language; English is a first-class alternative. Visitors arrive with an operational problem, not a technology shopping list.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The page must explain four capabilities, domain expertise, project versus dedicated-team engagement models, a delivery approach, case studies, team context, a secondary technology stack, and a contact or scheduling path. It must not show exact prices, margins, competitor rates, internal capacity, invented clients, invented metrics, or fabricated testimonials. Case studies use clearly marked placeholders until real evidence is supplied. The page is mobile-first, semantic, keyboard accessible, and targets AA contrast plus Lighthouse 95+ performance and accessibility.

## Brand Commitments

Name: HELIX. Existing reference: dark deep navy slides with blue, violet, pink, and green accents; Montserrat plus Open Sans. The new system may strengthen those references but must preserve the dark-first identity and use a subtle double-helix motif as identity, never as oversized decoration. Tone is concrete, operational, bilingual, and confident without hype.

## Evidence on Hand

No verified client logos, case-study details, testimonials, team portraits, metrics, or production screenshots were provided. The build must label synthetic operational diagrams and clearly mark every replacement item with [PLACEHOLDER].

## Product Principles

- Start from the operational problem, not the technology.
- Make integration and delivery tangible through specific workflow examples.
- Show enough engineering depth to earn trust without turning the page into a stack catalog.
- Keep the next action obvious: schedule a conversation or request a project quote.
- Treat Spanish and English as equal product experiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Spanish is the default language with a real English alternate. The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, AA contrast in dark and light modes, reduced motion, readable long copy, and mobile safe areas.
